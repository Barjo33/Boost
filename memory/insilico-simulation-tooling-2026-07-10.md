**2026-07-10: web research + build for "run the two engines side-by-side in accelerated time to get counterfactual BG."** Motivated by the shadow counterfactual-BG wall (we can log per-cycle would-doses but not the resulting BG — no simulator).

**Research findings (agent-verified):**
- **ReplayBG** (`gcappon/py_replay_bg`, Python, GPL-3.0, active v1.4.0 Jun-2026) — the ONLY tool for real-data counterfactual: fits a personalized model from a user's real CGM+insulin+CHO, replays with modified therapy → counterfactual BG. **BUT requires ALL carbs labelled; does NOT infer unannounced meals** — they get silently absorbed into a residual held fixed during replay, so counterfactual over unannounced-meal windows is unreliable-and-won't-announce-it. Our +12–38mg/dL unannounced-meal residual = the median case, so ReplayBG is directional-at-best on our population. Also validated only for ±20% therapy changes (V7-vs-V6 hard divergence is out of envelope) and only model-vs-model (UVA/Padova), never vs held-out real closed-loop.
- **simglucose** (`jxx123/simglucose`, MIT, py, semi-dormant) — UVA/Padova 2008 reimpl, 30 SYNTHETIC FDA patients, gym env. Can't replay real data. Good for virtual-patient behaviour/safety stress-test.
- UVA/Padova T1DMS/DMMS.R = commercial, virtual-patient. py-mgipsim = virtual+models physical activity. No AID-community retrospective BG-replay harness exists.
- **Verdict: nothing cleanly solves real-user A/B (unannounced carbs are fatal to individualized replay); the field uses within-user RCTs for the definitive answer** (hence the night-mode pre-reg A/B is the right instrument for that question).
