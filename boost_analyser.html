Boost Analyser — V1 vs V6, on your own data

Every Boost build since the multi-user shadow beta logs both engines' decisions every cycle: what your running engine delivered, and what the other engine decided on identical inputs. This page reads that telemetry straight from your Nightscout and shows you the real, paired comparison.

What this is — and is not. This compares what the two engines decided on your real history (doses, meal detection, timing). It does not simulate what your glucose would have been — insulin you didn't actually receive would have changed the BG that followed, and no physiologic model is used here. Glycaemia stats shown are your actual results on the engine you were running.
Privacy: this page has no server. Your URL and token are used only by your browser to call your Nightscout directly; nothing is sent anywhere else. Use a read-only token (Nightscout → Admin tools → Subjects, role readable).

Notes & caveats

  • Decision-level comparison only — see the banner at the top. For the validation methodology behind Boost changes (replay before ship), see backtesting/README.md in the repo.
  • Cycles are deduplicated to 5-minute buckets (AAPS re-invokes more often during SMB delivery).
  • On V6-active builds, V1's dose is parsed from the reason text (base would=…); cycles where that isn't present are counted as unpaired and excluded from dose deltas.
  • If your build predates the shadow telemetry (~May 2026), no boostV5_* fields exist and this page can't compare — update Boost first.
  • TING = Time In Normo-glycaemia, 3.5–7.8 mmol/L (63–140 mg/dL), reported alongside standard TIR 70–180.
